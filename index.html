Privacy Policy

Top Bins: Free Kick Soccer, by bestmakerdev. Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information Top Bins: Free Kick Soccer ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game shows ads and has no account registration.

Information we collect

  • An anonymous player ID. When you open the game, Unity Authentication creates a random ID for your device. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Game progress. Your coin balance, the cosmetics you own and basic reward timing (to apply daily limits) are stored in Unity Cloud Save under your anonymous ID.
  • Purchase records. If you buy coins, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a purchase receipt, which we check and store the transaction ID so that each purchase is credited only once. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • Online match data. In online matches, your aim, shots, equipped cosmetics and preset quick-chat messages are sent to your opponent through Unity's multiplayer services. Your IP address is used by these services to connect the match. There is no free-text chat.
  • Advertising data. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language, approximate location (from your IP address) and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalised.
  • Settings on your device. Volume, graphics and equipped items are saved locally on your device only.

How we use it

  • To save your coins and items and restore them on your device.
  • To verify purchases and prevent fraud or cheating.
  • To run online matches.
  • To show ads, which keep the game free.

We do not sell your information. Apart from Google AdMob, which collects advertising data as described above, we only share information with the service providers below.

Service providers

  • Unity Technologies (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Multiplayer): Unity privacy policy
  • Google AdMob (ads): how Google uses data in ads
  • Google Play (purchases): Google privacy policy

Your ad choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where required by law, such as in the EEA, UK and some US states, the game will ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress is kept while you play. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing us (address below) with the request "Delete my Top Bins data". Uninstalling the game removes local settings; because your ID is anonymous, include your purchase order number if you'd like us to locate your cloud data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It collects no names, contact details or free-text messages from anyone. Ads are set to be suitable for a general audience.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Unity's cloud services. Only our server code can change your coin balance.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
